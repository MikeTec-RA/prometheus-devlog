## The game

*Forgotten Memories, Endless Abyss* is an online fantasy MMO about exploring an immense
dungeon, recovering lost memories and living with the consequences of a shared world. The
dungeon reshapes itself around how its players behave, and it grows as they explore it.

The name goes back to a game idea of mine from college. Its Steam page still shows the old
project; once there's a game to talk about, it will carry this one.

## The engine

**Prometheus** is the custom engine the game is being built on. The world is fully 3D, and the
characters and props in it are 2D pixel-art sprites: that's the 2.5D. It comes with its own
tools:

- **The editor** for building and play-testing scenes, currently in the browser and moving
  to a desktop app
- **Frieze**, which turns 3D models into stylized sprite sheets
- **Forge**, a companion workspace for version control, code review, issue tracking and the
  project's documentation

The stack is open source throughout: Three.js and React in the editor, Rapier for physics,
and a Node backend with MySQL.

## Who's making it

Prometheus grew out of a scrapped avatar project that a friend and I worked on for about a year.
He built much of its animation system and some of the engine, and what we could salvage became
the starting point for this one: the [story is here](posts/2026-07-20-reforged-from-the-ashes/).

I've built Prometheus solo since then, with help on the way. My teenagers are studying
engineering and will, I hope, help design and build the game world. A few friends are taking on
the music and ambience.
