A guided tour of every feature

October 5, 2026 · 1 min read

Contents
  1. Ten stations
  2. The dry run caught three mistakes

Every feature that lands goes into one showcase scene, Re-Forged. By this month it had become a pile: lights, particles, sprites, widgets and physics props all fighting over the same patch of floor. So I rebuilt it as a tour.

Ten stations

It's now a hall 40 metres wide and 140 long, with numbered stations down its length, each with a sign: a welcome, then sprites and paths, behaviour trees, scripting, physics, lighting, particles, UI widgets, shared simulation, and a wall of materials. A saved camera flight weaves between them, so the whole tour plays as a fly-through.

The dry run caught three mistakes

I didn't build the new layout straight into the saved scene. A script built the proposed version as a private copy, flew a camera to each station and took a picture, without saving anything. Those pictures caught three problems:

  • Invisible signs. They stood outside the camera's view from the walking line, so nobody would ever have read them. They moved five metres in.
  • A moonlit wall. The three spotlights on the brick wall were 30 candela each, eight metres away. That works out to roughly the light of two full moons. Atmospheric, but not what a showcase wall needs. They went up to 140.
  • A distracted camera. The fly-through was pointing at the wrong places, and now looks at the stations.

At the physics station the spawner dropped four balls while the scene played, and none were left after Stop, so stopping really does put the world back. Then the layout was saved, and the same camera run against the saved scene gave the same pictures.
